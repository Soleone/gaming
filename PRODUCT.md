# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

A personal, curated video-game release calendar combining anticipation before playing and a short review afterward.

## Operating Context

Browse one year at a time, optionally narrowing to a month. Maintain selected games locally through a development-only editor. Production is read-only, built from the same JSON file.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Cover-led game cards grouped by month.
- Titles, covers (temporary art permitted), exact or partial release dates, and unknown dates.
- Hype rated 1–5; optional played reviews with scores 1–10.
- JSON is the source of truth. No database or production admin service.
- Initial selection: Castlevania: Belmont's Curse, Grand Theft Auto VI, The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake, and Phantom Blade Zero.
- Personal ratings are not supplied and must not be invented.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the Kit classic theme picker and use Gilt by default. Preserve Base Nova, semantic theme tokens, and stock vendored components.

## Product Principles

- Uncertain release dates stay uncertain.
- Keep personal opinion distinct from release facts.
- Make maintaining a small collection straightforward.
- Keep all themes usable, not just the default.
